# Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of personal data in connection with visits to this website and contacting us by email.

## 1. Controller

digitallabor.berlin · Deutscher Sparkassen- und Giroverband e.V. (DSGV) · Charlottenstraße 47, 10117 Berlin · Email: [info@digitallabor.berlin](mailto:info@digitallabor.berlin)

## 2. Purpose and legal basis

We process the data you voluntarily provide when contacting us (in particular name and email address) exclusively in order to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR or our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 3. Recipients

We do not pass your data to third parties unless this is necessary to fulfil statutory obligations or to respond to your enquiry.

## 4. Storage period

Your data will be deleted as soon as the purpose of the processing ceases to apply or you revoke your consent, unless statutory retention obligations require otherwise.

## 5. Revocation

You may revoke your consent at any time by email to [info@digitallabor.berlin](mailto:info@digitallabor.berlin). The lawfulness of processing carried out up to the revocation remains unaffected.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (for the DSGV: the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information).

Full provider information is available in the [Imprint]({{ '/imprint.html' | relative_url }}).
